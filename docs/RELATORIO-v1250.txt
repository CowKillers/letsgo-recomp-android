================================================================
CAUSA IDENTIFICADA
================================================================

O launcher ficava em tela cheia, mas ao abrir a LetsGoActivity as
barras de status e navegacao reapareciam.

Causa raiz: g_settings.fullscreen tem default FALSE (settings.c:20).
Por isso device.c:848 nunca executava:
  if (g_settings.fullscreen && ...) SDL_SetWindowFullscreen(window, true);

Sem a chamada SDL_SetWindowFullscreen, o SDLActivity nunca recebia
COMMAND_CHANGE_WINDOW_STYLE para esconder as barras via
WindowInsetsController. A applyFullscreen() da LetsGoActivity agia
sozinha e o SDL/sistema revertiam.

================================================================
ALTERACOES
================================================================

1. src/runtime/nvn/vulkan/device.c (ui_window_init)
   No Android (__ANDROID__), chama SDL_SetWindowFullscreen(window, true)
   incondicionalmente, com log do retorno e SDL_GetError():
     [vk] SDL_SetWindowFullscreen(true) -> ok / FAIL: <erro>
   Isso dispara COMMAND_CHANGE_WINDOW_STYLE no SDLActivity, que esconde
   as system bars via WindowInsetsController (API 30+) ou flags (API 28-29).

2. apk/java/com/letsgorecomp/LetsGoActivity.java
   - applyFullscreen(): usa WindowInsetsController (API 30+) com
     setDecorFitsSystemWindows(false) + hide(systemBars()) +

   - Logging diagnostico em applyFullscreen() e onWindowFocusChanged():

     [fs] WindowInsetsController: bars hidden, transient-by-swipe
     [fs] insets: statusBars=<top> navBars=<bottom>
     [fs] onWindowFocusChanged: hasFocus=<bool>
   - Reaplica em onCreate (apos super.onCreate), onResume e
     onWindowFocusChanged(true).

3. Preservado: orientacao corrigida (v1136), 720p interno
